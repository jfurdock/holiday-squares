# Holiday Squares

A tic-tac-toe survey party game for Thanksgiving and Christmas, played on a TV with a separate answer key for the host.

**Play:** https://jfurdock.github.io/holiday-squares/
**Answer key (host only):** add `#answers` to the end of the address.

## Boards

**Thanksgiving:** The Feast · Count Your Blessings · Food Coma · The Pie Table
**Christmas:** Under the Tree · Deck the Halls · Jingle All the Way · Santa's Workshop

Each board has 30 ranked answers and nine squares that split ranks #1–30.

## How to play

1. Split the room into two teams. Pick a holiday and a game on the TV menu, then choose who goes first.
2. On their turn, a team names one answer. Its rank decides its square. If the square is open, they take it.
3. A better-ranked answer steals the other team's square or upgrades your own. The top answer in a range locks the square.
4. Three in a row wins. If the board fills first, the team with more squares wins.

## Hosting

- Put the game on the TV (extend your display, don't mirror it), then click **Open answer key window** and keep that window on your laptop.
- Tap the answer a team says and it scores right away. **Not on list** passes the turn, and **Undo** reverses the last move.
- The two windows stay in sync when they're open in the same browser on the same computer. Everything works offline once the page has loaded.
